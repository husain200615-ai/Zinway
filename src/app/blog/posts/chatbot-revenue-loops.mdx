# Designing WhatsApp Revenue Loops with Conversational Intelligence

Most chatbot flows fail because they optimize for answers, not outcomes.

Our production framework maps user intent into three lanes:

- **Qualification** for relevance.
- **Recommendation** for confidence.
- **Conversion** for action.

When each lane is instrumented with event tracking, the bot becomes a revenue channel instead of support overhead.
